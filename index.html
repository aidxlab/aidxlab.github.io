About the
lab

Peter Faber Business School
Faculty of Law and Business
North Sydney

We bring together expertise from AI, business, law, ethics, education and information systems to develop trustworthy, human-centred and explainable AI solutions.

The AI for Decision Excellence (AIDX) Lab at the Peter Faber Business School, Faculty of Law and Business, Australian Catholic University (ACU) is an interdisciplinary research lab focused on the design, evaluation, and responsible use of artificial intelligence to improve important decisions made by people, organisations, and communities.

Our research advances decision intelligence, human-AI collaboration, agentic AI, responsible governance, and AI-enabled learning while ensuring that safety, fairness, privacy, and accountability remain central to innovation. Through research, education, and industry partnerships, we translate cutting-edge AI into practical solutions that create measurable social, organisational, and educational impact.
